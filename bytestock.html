Bytestock

Bytestock needed a brand that reflected where the business is today, not where it started. I led a full visual overhaul, creating a modern identity that feels at home in the tech industry while staying true to the company's values. From the logo and motion design to social media, email, print and large-scale exhibition graphics, every touchpoint was redesigned to create a more consistent and recognisable brand.

Live Site

Brief

Bring Bytestock's brand up to date and give it an identity that feels at home in today's tech industry.

Process

I rebuilt the brand from the ground up, creating a new logo and animated identity, updated colours, social templates, email design, and everything from banners to large-scale exhibition graphics.

Outcome

A modern, consistent brand that gives Bytestock a stronger presence across digital and print, making the business feel more credible, recognisable, and aligned with the tech world.

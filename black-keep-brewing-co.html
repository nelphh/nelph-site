Black Keep Brewing Co.

Black Keep Brewing Co. is a concept craft beer brand built around a modern take on medieval storytelling. The aim was to create a bold and memorable identity that combines historic influences with a contemporary craft beer aesthetic. I developed the brand identity around the flagship beer, Sentinel, creating a visual world through logo design, illustration and packaging.

Brief

Create a distinctive craft beer brand that reimagines medieval themes for a modern audience, combining bold visuals with a premium and memorable identity.

Process

I developed the Black Keep Brewing Co. identity through a custom logo, medieval-inspired typography and detailed illustrations. For the Sentinel beer range, I created a bold can design using strong colours and graphic elements to bring the brand story to life while keeping it relevant within the modern craft beer market.

Outcome

A unique and recognisable craft beer identity that blends medieval character with contemporary design. The strong visual system creates a memorable product presence and gives Black Keep Brewing Co. a clear foundation for future beers and brand extensions.

Sports Design

Football illustration and design is a personal passion project where I explore the connection between sport, identity and visual storytelling. Focusing mainly on the England national team and my local club Queens Park Rangers, I create artwork that captures moments, players and the culture surrounding the game. These projects allow me to experiment with illustration styles, typography and graphic design while creating pieces that feel authentic to the fans and communities behind the clubs.

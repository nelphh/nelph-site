Step N Flex

Step N Flex is a modern, high-energy step class built around rhythm, movement and community. I created a bold identity that reflects the atmosphere of the classes, giving the brand a confident visual presence across digital and physical platforms.

Brief

Create a recognisable fitness brand that feels energetic, empowering and modern, helping Step N Flex stand out within the fitness space.

Process

I developed a bold logo system using the “N” as a flexible graphic asset, allowing it to be reused across social content, posters, merchandise and branded materials. A purple, navy and white colour palette was introduced to create impact while keeping the brand feeling contemporary and premium.

Outcome

A strong and consistent identity that gives Step N Flex a recognisable presence across every touchpoint. The flexible logo and visual system help build brand awareness while creating a more engaging experience for its community.
